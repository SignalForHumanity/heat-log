NYC heat log check

Have a thermometer that records the temperature and exports a CSV file (SensorPush, Govee, ThermoPro, Inkbird and others do)? This page checks every hour of it against New York City's heat law and makes a log you can print for 311, HPD, a lawyer, or Housing Court.

Your file never leaves this device. It is read and checked here, in your browser.

What the law requires (October 1 to May 31)
  • 6am to 10pm: at least 68°F inside whenever it is below 55°F outside.
  • 10pm to 6am: at least 62°F inside, whatever the weather.

NYC Housing Maintenance Code §27-2029. Hot water (120°F at the tap, all year) is not covered here.

1. Your thermometer's file

Put the thermometer in the coldest room you use, away from windows, radiators and the floor.

Optional: for the printed log

Only printed on this page; not saved or sent anywhere.

2. Outdoor temperature (for the daytime rule)

This sends only the first and last date of your log to open-meteo.com. These are modelled temperatures, not an official weather-station record; for court, ask for NOAA's certified Central Park data.

Without either, night hours are still checked; daytime hours below 68°F are marked "possible".

Heat log

Each hour uses the average of that hour's readings. Thermometers are usually accurate to about ±1°F, so keep yours where it can't be disputed, and keep the original file.

Getting help

  • Report no heat to 311; ask for the complaint number.
  • Met Council on Housing's tenants' rights hotline and its paper heat log.
  • Free legal help: NYC tenant legal services.
